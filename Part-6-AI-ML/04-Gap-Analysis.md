# Gap Analysis

## Target gap
Given many maintenance tasks and operational constraints, determine which tasks should be scheduled, when, where, and in what coordinated block arrangement to minimize disruption while meeting maintenance needs.

## Differentiation
- Evaluate competing requests together
- Calculate feasible windows
- Multi-constraint conflict analysis
- Feasibility-aware coordination
- Ranked alternative schedules
- What-if comparison
- Explain why a recommendation was selected

Do not claim that every listed capability is absent from Indian Railways. Validate final claims against official sources.
