# AI/ML Design

## Optimization
Core scheduling engine: constraint satisfaction and objective optimization.

## Optional ML
- Maintenance urgency prediction
- Traffic/load forecasting
- Maintenance duration estimation
- Delay-risk prediction

## Optional LLM
- Explain recommendations
- Convert natural-language planner requests into filters
- Summarize weekly plans

For the MVP, prioritize a strong optimization engine. Do not claim ML accuracy without validated historical data.
